# Community conduct

Effective 17 November 2026. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests,
discussions, events and people representing cypherpunk_vpn, including partnership
decisions. It does not add conditions to Apache-2.0 or govern private use of the VPN.

Treat people with respect. Technical disagreement, criticism of designs and correction
of unsupported claims are welcome. Racism, fascism, discriminatory exclusion,
harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, sexual harassment and the trivialization of
historical or current violence are incompatible with project participation. Do not
retaliate against someone for reporting a concern in good faith.

## Private reports and responsibility

The responsible maintainer is **R1VER77**, the repository owner. Send a private conduct
report through the owner's [SimpleX contact](https://smp16.simplex.im/a#i_Mw0Ivin12L1_Gbr_LmydeNtxuDzkPW_eOyTM10q-8).
For a software vulnerability use the separate [security reporting route](SECURITY.md).
Include only the relevant interaction, when and where it occurred, what response you
seek, and a safe way to reply. A pseudonym is sufficient; do not send identity documents,
keys, PINs, wallet material or VPN activity logs. Screenshots and names are optional.

The maintainer should acknowledge reports within seven days when available, explain
any delay, restrict access to those handling the report, and avoid sharing identifying
information without consent unless needed to address an immediate safety risk. This
is a volunteer project, not an emergency service. Delete report material when no longer
needed for handling and appeals; repository-host and communication-service retention
remain outside the project's direct control.

## Response and appeal

Assess the evidence, context, severity, repeated behavior and risk of retaliation.
Where safe, give the person concerned a chance to respond. Responses may include a
request to stop, removal of content, a warning, temporary participation limits or
exclusion. State the reason, scope and duration privately to affected people; do not
publish a reporter's identity. Immediate temporary limits may be used while a serious
report is assessed. Apply the same standards to maintainers and contributors.

Either party may request reconsideration through the same private route, provide
additional evidence and ask for an uninvolved reviewer. The maintainer must disclose
their conflict and recuse from assessing reports about their own conduct. This project
currently has one responsible maintainer and no standing independent appeal panel;
an appeal needing another reviewer remains pending until a willing, unconflicted
person acceptable to the affected parties is available. Do not describe the original
maintainer's reconsideration as an independent appeal. Document the final reason and
any change in outcome privately. Publish only anonymized process improvements when safe.

No conduct investigation authorizes VPN traffic inspection, user tracking or a change
to the software license. The policy follows the project's [principles](docs/PRINCIPLES.md)
and is open to review through [governance](docs/GOVERNANCE.md).
