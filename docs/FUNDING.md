# Voluntary funding and partnership record

Updated 17 November 2026. The owner intends to disclose funding if or when it is
obtained. This is a voluntary transparency commitment, not a statement that the owner
has a legal disclosure duty.

| Date | Owner-reported position | Development authorization |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-17 | No funding or sponsorship to report at this time; future funding will be disclosed by the owner. | Continue using existing local resources and free tools; no paid audit, new subscription or cloud spend authorized. |

This is an owner-reported record, not an audited financial statement, donor registry
or proof of independence. Existing personal equipment, unpaid work and provider free
tiers do not establish an independent relay operator or independent security review.
No external auditor or audit budget is currently appointed in this record. Review
can be unpaid; development does not depend on obtaining funding.

For future support, record its date, cash or in-kind nature, material conditions,
amount or a useful range where appropriate, relevant conflicts and resulting project
decisions. Publish a sponsor's name only with authorization; private donors may be
described in aggregate without identifiers. Record meaningful influence or conflicts
even when an identity remains private. A missing entry must never become a claim that
no relationship exists. The maintainer owns updates and correction of this record.

Partnership decisions must consider the [principles](PRINCIPLES.md),
[conduct policy](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), privacy impact, technical independence and
any condition affecting security claims. Record the reasoning through
[governance](GOVERNANCE.md). Sponsorship does not purchase approval of security claims,
access to user traffic, proprietary license conditions or control of user keys.
