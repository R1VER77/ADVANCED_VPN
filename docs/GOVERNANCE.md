# Governance and decisions

Updated 17 November 2026. **R1VER77** is the current owner and responsible maintainer.
This is a maintainer-led project; it has no elected board, nonprofit status established
by these documents, independent appeal panel or independent security auditor.

Anyone may propose a change through an issue or pull request, using a pseudonym.
Describe the problem, proposed behavior, privacy impact, affected platforms and
evidence needed. Review the code and evidence rather than a person's credentials or
status. Small corrections may use the commit or pull-request description as their
record. Changes to trust boundaries, defaults, compatibility, keys, claims, governance
or funding conditions need a dated decision in [the decision log](DECISIONS.md), with
the reason, evidence, limits, acceptance gate and a way to reopen the decision.

The maintainer decides acceptance and records unresolved objections. Anyone can ask
for reconsideration by citing new evidence or a failed acceptance gate. When a conflict
affects a decision, disclose it and seek unconflicted review; do not present a sole
maintainer's decision as independent consensus. Security-sensitive reports stay private
until coordinated disclosure under [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

Changes require appropriate checks and verified commit signatures. The owner's standing
instruction authorizes automatic publication and integration of signed commits, subject
to repository protections and required checks. Preserve signed history and avoid force
pushes. Record the resulting published commit and CI result. Publication of source is
separate from installing it on a live VPN, spending money or changing hardware secrets.

Maintain [principles](PRINCIPLES.md), [conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md),
[voluntary funding disclosures](FUNDING.md), [roadmap gates](ROADMAP.md), release notes
and incident evidence in public, with personal and operational secrets removed.
Policies govern participation and project decisions; the software is licensed under
AGPL-3.0-or-later (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)). They do not authorize monitoring VPN users.
