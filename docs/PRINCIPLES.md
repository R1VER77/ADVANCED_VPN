# Project principles

Adopted 17 November 2026. cypherpunk_vpn is free software for private communication,
with public designs and evidence that people can inspect, reproduce and challenge.

1. **Privacy by construction.** Minimize what each component learns. Avoid identity
   collection, telemetry and traffic retention as prerequisites for using the software.
   Explain what providers, operators and compromised endpoints can still observe.
2. **User autonomy.** Keep bring-your-own servers, locally held keys, explicit actions,
   reversible setup and documented recovery. Installation never silently activates a
   tunnel. Disconnect and failure must not silently remove an active traffic guard.
3. **Decentralization with evidence.** Separate operators and failure domains where
   possible. More machines owned by one person do not establish operator independence
   or an anonymity set. Report real deployment limitations.
4. **Software freedom and shared knowledge.** Preserve AGPL-3.0-or-later dual-licensing with hardware exceptions and public
   protocol documentation, tests and negative findings. Judge contributions by their
   substance, evidence and conduct; credentials, employer and status are not entry tests.
5. **Accessible control.** Aim for clear progress, keyboard access and recoverable
   failures. Explain server, hardware, connectivity and cover-traffic costs. Measure
   usability and energy use before claiming accessibility or mobile suitability.
6. **Social responsibility.** Consider how design choices affect people beyond the
   immediate user. Reject racism, fascism, harassment and the trivialization of violence
   in project participation and partnerships. Apply the [conduct policy](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
   without inspecting users' traffic or adding restrictions to the software license.
7. **Honest assurance.** Keep implemented, simulated, physically tested and independently
   reviewed claims distinct. Publish incidents and limits, including unresolved traffic
   analysis leakage. AI-generated work and passing CI are not independent security review.

These are this project's commitments, informed by the CCC's
[Hacker Ethics](https://www.ccc.de/en/hackerethik),
[statutes](https://www.ccc.de/en/satzung) and
[incompatibility declaration](https://www.ccc.de/en/club/unvereinbarkeitserklaerung-des-ccc).
We claim no CCC affiliation, endorsement or certification. The CCC's own association
structure and nonprofit rules do not establish this project's legal structure.
